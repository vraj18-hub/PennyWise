About this project

PennyWise is a personal finance coach built to explain financial concepts clearly and give personalized guidance grounded in your own spending — without pretending to be a licensed financial advisor.

How it's built

Every answer comes from a retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) pipeline: your question is matched against a curated knowledge base using embeddings and a vector database, and the retrieved passages are handed to a language model along with your own transaction summary — never raw transaction data.

FastAPI SQLite + SQLAlchemy Argon2 password hashing JWT sessions Chroma vector database Sentence-Transformers embeddings Local LLM (Ollama)

Why it looks like a bank

Rather than a generic dashboard, PennyWise is designed as a walk-through of a real bank: a gate to sign in at, and desks inside for each service — a ledger for your spending, an advisor for finance questions, a coach for personalized guidance, and a vault for your privacy settings.

A note on advice

This is an educational project, not a substitute for a licensed financial advisor. Always verify important financial decisions with a qualified professional.
