Privacy Policy

Last updated for this project demo.

What we store

  • Your email and a securely hashed version of your password (never the password itself)
  • The transactions you upload: date, description, amount, and category
  • Nothing else — no bank account numbers, no card numbers, no government IDs

What we never do

  • We never send your raw transaction list to the AI model — only aggregated totals (like "food: ₹78.25")
  • We never connect directly to your real bank account
  • We never log your password or full financial details in server logs
You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from The Vault.

Not financial advice

PennyWise provides general educational information and does not replace advice from a licensed financial advisor. Always verify important decisions independently.
