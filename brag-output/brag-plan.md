# PennyWise — /brag Launch Video Plan

## Overview
- **Project**: PennyWise — Local AI Personal Finance Coach
- **Format**: Landscape 1920x1080, 30fps
- **Target Duration**: ~22 seconds
- **Tone**: `polished` (Sleek, elegant, refined bank aesthetic)

## The Core Angle & Hook
- **Hook**: "Personal finance where raw transactions never leave your machine."
- **Visual Identity**: Warm marble, rich wood, brass accents, Fraunces serif typography.
- **Key Progression**:
  1. The Gate (Argon2 Sign In)
  2. The Lobby (Interactive 4-Desk Navigation)
  3. The Ledger Desk (Fast CSV parsing & financial breakdown)
  4. The Advisor's Desk (Curated RAG search with source citations)
  5. The Coach's Corner (Personalized AI coach powered by local Llama 3.2)
  6. The Vault (Total data sovereignty & right to erasure)
  7. Outro (Open Source, Local-First, Private)
